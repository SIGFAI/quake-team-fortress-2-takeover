# Credits
All art is generated for this mod with fal (nano-banana-2 concept images, trellis-2 image-to-3D models, elevenlabs sound effects and voice lines), then converted by the kit tools:
- Heavy, Scout, Soldier, Pyro, sentry gun, top hat: models from original generated concept art.
- Sandvich sprite: generated image, cleaned and palettized.
- Sounds: generated (minigun, flame, crit chime, sentry, bat, hat pop, munch, fanfare, mercenary voice lines).
Base data: LibreQuake (BSD) and id Software QuakeC 1.06 (GPL). No assets from Team Fortress 2 or any commercial game.
